How to Play

Guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries.

Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word. After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was:

W
E
A
R
Y
  • Green The letter is in the word and in the correct spot.
  • Yellow The letter is in the word but in the wrong spot.
  • Gray The letter is not in the word at all.

Keyboard: Type using your physical keyboard or tap the on-screen keys. Press Enter to submit and Backspace to delete.
